# Design Decisions

## Direction A: Clean Professional

- **Style:** Minimal, professional, white background
- **Colors:** Blue accent (#2563EB)
- **Typography:** Inter, clean hierarchy
- **Layout:** Single-column, generous whitespace
- **Target:** SMB decision-makers
- **Score:** 43/50

## Direction B: Modern Tech-Forward

- **Style:** Dark sections + white sections, subtle gradients
- **Colors:** Blue-to-purple gradient
- **Typography:** Inter, bold headlines
- **Layout:** Split layouts where appropriate
- **Target:** Technical decision-makers
- **Score:** 45/50

## Provisional Recommendation

**Direction B (Modern Tech-Forward)** — highest visual quality, workflow visibility, and portfolio value.

Final selection pending CEO and UI designer review.

## Content Decisions

- Hero headline uses "Turn Website Enquiries Into a Reliable Lead Workflow" (not "Automate Your Lead Pipeline")
- Lead form uses 9 fields (not 5)
- Connected Workflow shows all 6 steps (not "Design → Automate → Convert")
- FAQ uses controlled scope (no fixed timeline promises, no "fully customizable")
- No unverified statistics
- No excessive animations (bouncing, pulsing)
